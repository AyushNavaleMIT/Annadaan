How the model works

Trained on 600 historical records across 3 years (2022–2024)

01
Historical baseline
Each shelter has per-month averages and standard deviations derived from real service data, giving a realistic baseline for any date of the year.
02
Day-type uplift
Weekends see 21.9% more visitors than weekdays. Festival days like Diwali, Holi, and Republic Day drive 66.4% higher turnout — both uplifts are applied when relevant.
03
Food calculation
Each shelter has a calibrated food-per-person ratio (~0.21–0.22 kg). Multiply by the predicted headcount to get kilograms required and the number of 500g packs to prepare.
